Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our railway tracking engine.

How does Aagman predict train ETA? ▼
Aagman combines historical transit curves across thousands of journeys with real-time GPS coordinates, station clearance reports, weather sensor telemetry, and dynamic section headway constraints to forecast arrival times with over 96% accuracy.
How often is train position updated? ▼
Train positions are updated every 30 to 60 seconds using live GPS beacons and network telemetry, ensuring the delay verdict reflects real-time realities.
What does "Delayed 12 min" mean? ▼
This indicates that based on current speed and network traffic, the train is projected to arrive at the next station 12 minutes later than its officially scheduled time.
Why can the predicted ETA change? ▼
Predictions are dynamic. If a train makes up time by running at higher speeds on a clear track section, or encounters unexpected congestion, the ETA will instantly recalculate.
What do the different colors and status indicators mean? ▼
Green means on time, Red means delayed, Blue indicates the immediate upcoming station, and Gray shows stations further down the route. The dark navy beacon shows exact current position.
How accurate are the predictions? ▼
Our machine learning models are continuously trained on live data, achieving an accuracy rate of 96% for predictions made within 3 hours of arrival.
